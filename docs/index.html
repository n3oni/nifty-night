Nifty Night — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 1 June 2026

Nifty Night is a simple sleep-timer app. It is built to respect your privacy: it does not collect, store, share, or transmit any personal information.

Information we collect

None. Nifty Night does not collect any personal data. There are no user accounts, no sign-in, no analytics, no advertising, and no tracking of any kind.

Internet & data transmission

The app does not connect to the internet and does not send any data off your device. Everything it does happens locally on your phone.

Permissions we use

  • Notifications — used only to show the countdown while a sleep timer is running.
  • Device Admin (screen lock) — used only to lock your screen when the timer ends, if you choose to enable that option. This permission is used solely on your device and is never used to access, collect, or transmit any information.
  • Foreground service — used only to keep the timer running reliably while your phone settles for the night.

You can revoke the screen-lock permission at any time in your phone's settings, and the timer will still work without it.

Children's privacy

Nifty Night does not collect any data from anyone, including children.

Changes to this policy

If this policy ever changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new "last updated" date.

Contact

If you have any questions about this privacy policy, you can reach us via the Nifty Night project page on GitHub.
